1 · Questionnaire

Questions go to rfi-agent through the pp-joke plugin, 10 at a time; each batch takes a few minutes. The agent never searches or drafts red questions. It searches the library for green and amber ones but drafts only green. A green question with no usable match becomes amber and is routed. Works only in the Claude desktop app.
